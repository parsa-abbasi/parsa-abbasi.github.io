Our paper, [*CQD-SHAP: Explainable Complex Query Answering via Shapley Values*](/publication/cqd-shap/), has been accepted at [ECML PKDD 2026](https://ecmlpkdd.org/2026/) in Naples, Italy. Joint work with [Dr. Stefan Heindorf](https://www.uni-paderborn.de/person/11871).

I will present the work on September 9 at 12:05 in the session *"Structured Graphs, Causal & Relational Learning."* The poster session will take place the same day, from 7 to 9 PM.
